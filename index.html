Questions, answered once

Frequently asked — by owners like you.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You tell me what your business needs; I build it, connect it, and hand it over working. If something needs changing later, that's a message to me, not a support ticket.

Can customers actually pay online, not just book?

Yes — deposits or full payment at the time of booking, through a standard payment processor. Money lands in your account; you're never holding a card number.

I already have a website. Can this replace just part of it?

Often, yes. Scheduling, payments, and FAQ can be added to an existing site, or we can rebuild the whole thing if it's due for one anyway.

What if my services or prices change?

You send me the update, I make the change. No logins to remember, no page builder to fight with.

How long does it take to go live?

Most sites launch within two to three weeks of the discovery call, depending on how much content and how many services need to be set up.
